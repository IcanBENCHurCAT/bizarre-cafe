## Pose & Vibe

- Standing front-facing or slightly angled toward viewer
- **One arm open wide** (welcoming gesture)
- **One hand on hip** (confident, casual)
- Leaning in slightly toward the viewer
- Face lit up with big smile, eyes crinkled at corners
- Body language screams "come sit down, let me tell you a story"
- **Warm golden/amber lighting** — like sunset through a window or warm bar lighting

## Style Notes for Generation

1. **Two hands only** — this is non-negotiable
2. The jean jacket should look **lived-in and comfortable**, not brand-new or expensive
3. The beard should be **thick and full**, not a designer stubble
4. The watches are **stacked on one wrist** (left), all clearly visible
5. The gold chain should be **prominent but not excessive**
6. Expression is **joyful storytelling**, not posing for a photo
7. Lighting is **warm and golden**, creating a welcoming, inviting feel
8. Overall aesthetic: **retro marketing poster** — bold, vibrant, character-forward, slightly exaggerated but grounded in reality

## DO NOT Include

- No fantasy/sci-fi elements (no cybernetics, no magic)
- No sunglasses (his face should be fully visible)
- No cap or hat
- No visible tattoos
- No more or fewer than 2 hands
- No futuristic or neon clothing
- No formal wear (no suits, ties, dress shirts)
